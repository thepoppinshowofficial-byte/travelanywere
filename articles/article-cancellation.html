Key things to consider

  • Find the cancellation deadline.
  • Check whether terms are per booking or traveler.
  • Save a copy of terms.
  • Do not assume flexibility is unlimited.

Verify before you travel

Schedules, prices, availability, entry requirements and provider policies can change. Confirm important details with the relevant provider or official authority.
