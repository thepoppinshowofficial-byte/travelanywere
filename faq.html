TravelAnywere FAQ

Answers about our content, providers and affiliate links.

Does TravelAnywere sell tickets?

No. TravelAnywere provides content and links to third-party providers. Bookings are made with the provider after you leave our site.

Are prices guaranteed?

No. Prices and availability can change. Verify the final price and terms on the provider's booking page.

Do you earn from links?

Some links are affiliate links. A qualifying booking may generate a commission without changing the customer's price.

Are you officially connected to the brands?

Not necessarily. Brand names identify the third-party provider; we do not claim official representation merely by linking to them.

Can you change or cancel my booking?

No. Contact the provider shown on your confirmation.
